Case Study — Sea / Shopee

Turning marketing chaos into a 98%-accurate agent

Applied AI Agent Engineer · 2025
The situation

Shopee's marketing operations were drowning in manual work: 11 MKT datasets to reconcile by hand, spike-day queries piling up, and policy checks needed across 5 campaign types and 11 shipping channels before any campaign could safely go live.

What I built

A dual-track agent workflow that decomposes business intent and verifies live schema in parallel, plus a single-track agent for spike-day queries, an automation pipeline wired into Jira, and an evaluation pipeline built on Playwright.

98% accuracy−50% request volume~25 hrs/week saved~23 hrs/bi-week saved9 teams adopted~40 engineer-days saved
What I gained

How to translate an ambiguous business ask into a verifiable agent architecture — and that shipping the agent is the easy half. The hard half is getting nine different teams to actually trust it enough to adopt it, which is why I wrote the playbooks, not just the code.

Case Study — GatherEase AI

Being the only line between a client and a working product

Co-Founder, Front-End Engineer & Operations · 2024
The situation

A B2B event-management startup with one shot at a first client — no fallback team, no existing playbook. I owned the client-facing operational workflow end to end, from business model definition through first delivery.

What I built

I led the first-client market entry strategy, then designed and shipped the entire front end solo — Figma UX/UI straight into React — translating a client's raw needs into a working interface under real deadline pressure.

−1 hr operational latency−50% recruitment costsolo Figma → React build
What I gained

What it feels like when there's no one else to catch a dropped requirement. That pressure taught me to scope fast and correctly the first time — a habit that's shaped every agent I've shipped since.

Case Study — PicCollage

Learning that a "small" bug is a business signal

Quality Assurance Intern · 2023
The situation

A product with 200M+ users, where growth and stability were both non-negotiable — and where the gap between "annoying" and "churn-causing" friction is often invisible until you go looking for it.

What I built

I ran structured A/B tests across 150+ UX friction points, partnering directly with PM, SWE, and design to decide which fixes actually moved performance and growth KPIs.

150+ friction points tested200M+ user basecross-functional triage
What I gained

Triage is a judgment call, not a checklist. At that scale, deciding which friction point is worth fixing this sprint is the same muscle as deciding which agent is worth building — you're always trading a small signal against its compounding cost.

Case Study — UNSW

The first time I had to scope someone else's ambiguity

Student Consultant · 2021
The situation

Four real external clients — the Australian Maritime Museum, Kua, the Australian International Sports Organisation, and Grant Thornton — each with a vague ask and no corporate safety net if I got the scope wrong.

What I built

Directed market research across 20+ schools for the Maritime Museum, formulated a 3-lever growth strategy for Kua, ran SEO and 3,000+ survey research with frontend optimization for the sports org, and delivered Grant Thornton's ESG risk matrix and procurement methodology.

4 external clients20+ school survey3,000+ survey responses1st place, Grant Thornton case comp.
What I gained

This is where the muscle started: turning a client's vague ask into a scoped, deliverable plan. Everything I've built since — the Shopee agents, the GatherEase product — is that same muscle, just with more code attached.
