Sea / Shopee

Turning marketing chaos into a 98%-accurate agent

Applied AI Agent Engineer · 2025
The situation

Shopee's marketing operations were drowning in manual work: 11 MKT datasets to reconcile by hand, spike-day queries piling up, and policy checks needed across 5 campaign types and 11 shipping channels before any campaign could safely go live.

What I built

A dual-track agent workflow that decomposes business intent and verifies live schema in parallel, plus a single-track agent for spike-day queries, an automation pipeline wired into Jira, and an evaluation pipeline built on Playwright. This spanned both sides of the same problem: the data side (SQL, schema, 11 datasets) and the engineering side (API integrations, evaluation pipelines).

98% accuracy50% fewer requests~25 hrs/week saved~23 hrs/bi-week saved9 teams adopted~40 engineer-days saved
Why this matters for you

If your team is stuck trusting a spreadsheet over an agent, this is the muscle I bring: fluency on both the data side and the engineering side of the same system, so I can ship the agent and then earn nine different teams' trust in it, not just write the code.

GatherEase AI

Being the only line between a client and a working product

Co-Founder, Front-End Engineer & Operations · 2024
The situation

A B2B event-management startup with one shot at a first client, no fallback team, no existing playbook. I owned the client-facing operational workflow end to end, from business model definition through first delivery.

What I built

I led the first-client market entry strategy, then designed and shipped the entire front end solo: Figma UX/UI straight into React, translating a client's raw needs into a working interface under real deadline pressure.

1 hr latency saved50% recruitment cost savedsolo Figma → React build
Why this matters for you

If your product needs someone who can sit with a client, define the business model, and then build the interface themselves, that's this role. There was no one else to catch a dropped requirement, so I learned to scope fast and correctly the first time, a habit that's shaped every agent I've shipped since.

PicCollage

Learning that a "small" bug is a business signal

Quality Assurance Intern · 2023
The situation

A product with 200M+ users, where growth and stability were both non-negotiable, and where the gap between "annoying" and "churn-causing" friction is often invisible until you go looking for it.

What I built

I ran structured A/B tests across 150+ UX friction points, partnering directly with PM, SWE, and design to decide which fixes actually moved performance and growth KPIs.

150+ friction points tested200M+ user basecross-functional triage
Why this matters for you

If your team needs someone who can tell which small bug is actually a business risk, this is that instinct. At 200M-user scale, deciding what's worth fixing this sprint is the same judgment call as deciding which agent is worth building: you're always trading a small signal against its compounding cost.
